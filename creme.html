Recipe #4 Crème brûlée

  • Time: 4 hours
  • Serves: 4-6 servings
Ingredients Quantity
Eggs 6
white sugar 1/2
heavy whipping cream 1/2
vannilla extract 1 tsp

Step 1: Heat your oven to 325°F Place 4 to 6 flameproof ceramic ramekins inside a large, deep baking pan and set aside

Step 2: Whisk the egg yolks and ½ cup of sugar together in a bowl

Step 3: In a saucepan over medium heat, combine the heavy cream, vanilla, and salt. Bring it just to a simmer until tiny bubbles form around the edges,

Step 4: pour a small stream of the hot cream into the egg mixture while whisking constantly.

Step 5: Strain the liquid into your ramekins. Pour boiling water into the outer baking pan until it reaches halfway up the sides of the ramekins. Bake for 30 to 40 minutes

Step 6: Remove the ramekins from the water bath, let them cool to room temperature, and refrigerate for at least 4 hours
