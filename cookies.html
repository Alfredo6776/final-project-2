Recipe #5 Chocolate Cookies

  • Time: 35 minutes
  • Serves: 8-10 servings
Ingredients Quantity
Butter 1/2 cups
Brown sugar 1/2 cups
white sugar 1/2
egg 1
vanilla extract 2 tsp
flour 2 tsp
baking soda 4/4 tsp
salt 1/2 tsp
choclate 1 cup

Step 1: beat the softened butter, brown sugar, and white sugar with an electric mixer until light and creamy.

Step 2: Add the egg and vanilla extract to the butter mixture, beating until completely combined

Step 3: In a saucepan over medium heat, combine the heavy cream, vanilla, and salt. Bring it just to a simmer until tiny bubbles form around the edges,

Step 4: : In a separate bowl, whisk together the all-purpose flour, baking soda, and salt.

Step 5: Add the flour mixture to the wet ingredients in two separate batches, mixing on low speed until just combined.

Step 6: fold in the chocolate chips using a spatula or wooden spoon until evenly distributed.

Step 7: Scoop the dough into rounded tablespoon-sized balls and place them on a tray. Refrigerate the dough balls for 30–40 minutes.

Step 8: Bake for 115 to 20 minutes until golden.
