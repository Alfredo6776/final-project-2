Recipe #1 Brownies

  • Time: 45 minutes _
  • Serves: 9 servings _
Ingredients Quantity
unsalted butter half cup
sugar 1 cup
cocoa powder 1/2 cup
eggs 2
vanilla extract 1 tsp
all purpose flour 3/4 cup

Step 1: Heat oven to 350°F. Grease bottom and sides of baking pan and line it with parchment paper.

Step 2: Melt butter in the microwave for 30 seconds.Stir partially melted butter to allow fully melt the remaining butter.

Step 3: Whisk in sugar and cocoa. Mix in eggs and vanilla.

Step 4: Using a spatula, fold flour into the mixture until it is completely blended. Pour batter into the prepared pan.

Step 5: Bake for 35 to 40 minutes until ready to eat.

Recipe #2 Pretzals

  • Time: 12 minutes
  • Serves: 1 servings
Ingredients Quantity
Warm water 1.5 cups
Yeast 1 tsp
Salt tsp
Sugar tsp
Flour 4.5 cupps

Step 1: Activate yeast in warm water with sugar and salt for 5 minutes.

Step 2: Mix in flour and knead until a smooth dough forms.

Step 3: Rise the dough in a warm bowl for 1 hour.

Step 4: Shape dough into long ropes and twist into pretzel shapes.

Step 5: Boil each pretzel in the baking soda water for 15 seconds.

Step 6: Bake at 450°F for 10 to 12 minutes until golden.

Recipe #3 Cake

  • Time: 12 minutes
  • Serves: 1 servings
Ingredients Quantity
flour 1 1/2
sugar 1 cup
unsalted butter 1/2
whole milk 1/2
eggs 2
vanilla extract 2 tsp
baking soda 1 3/4
salt 1/4

Step 1: Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease and lightly flour on a cake pan.

Step 2: Beat the softened butter and sugar with a hand or stand mixer on medium-high speed for 3-4 minutes until pale and fluffy. Beat in the eggs one at a time, followed by the vanilla extract

Step 3: In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt. With your mixer on low, add about a third of the flour mixture to the butter mixture, followed by a splash of milk. Mix only until just combined to avoid a dense cake.

Step 4: Pour the batter into your prepared pan and smooth the top. Bake for 30–35.

Step 5: Let the cake cool in the pan for 10 minutes before eating.
